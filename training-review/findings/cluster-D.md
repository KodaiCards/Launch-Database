# Findings — Cluster D: Construction, Splicing & Testing (T10, T11, T12)

## Summary

- **Lessons reviewed:** 42 (T10: 12, T11: 15, T12: 15)
- **Overall:** Accuracy avg 4.1 / Pedagogy avg 4.2 / Engagement avg 4.0
- **Headline:** The cluster is technically strong with well-sourced content on TIA-598 color coding, splice-loss thresholds, and OTDR fundamentals. One safety-critical error in T10.L09 presents the MUTCD transition-taper formula incorrectly for ≥45 mph scenarios (result is 45× too small — a field-deployable error). The IEC 61300-3-35 Zone B boundary in T12.L11 is cited as 110 µm (2022 Edition 3), which is not confirmed in publicly accessible text of that standard; the common published boundary is 125 µm for the outer cladding region and 25 µm for Zone A inner. The RUS splice bulletin cite (`1753F-401`) appears consistent throughout but the form number should be double-checked against the current USDA RUS numbering. All other accuracy-critical facts — TIA-598 12-color sequence, fusion splice loss targets (≤0.10 dB FOA design target, ≤0.30 dB RUS contract max), cleave angle limits (≤0.5° target / ≤1.0° max), EIOR values for G.652.D, and dual-wavelength macrobend detection — are correctly stated. Pedagogy is consistently strong: worked examples with shown arithmetic, callback callouts, and Book vs. Field sections are model patterns. Engagement is competent and practical; the main gap is that T10 leans very prose-heavy relative to T11/T12.

## Top 10 Fixes for This Cluster (prioritized)

1. **❌ WRONG — T10.L09 (Traffic Control) — Fix the MUTCD taper formula for ≥45 mph.** The transition taper formula at 45 mph produces 9 ft instead of the correct 405 ft. This is a safety-critical error: a 9-ft taper at 45 mph is not a valid work zone design and would not pass any state DOT review. Fix: apply L = WS²/60 for the 45 mph example (result = 405 ft), or switch the example to a ≤45 mph scenario using L = WS/60 with correct result. Update the key_term, flashcard, and all three working-section occurrences.

2. **⚠️ RISKY — T12.L11 (End-Face Inspection) — Verify Zone B outer boundary against IEC 61300-3-35:2022 Ed.3.** The lesson asserts 110 µm (2022) vs. 115 µm (pre-2022); public secondary sources vary (some say 120 µm). Obtain the primary standard or cite a verifiable secondary. If the 110 µm value cannot be confirmed, revert to "per IEC 61300-3-35:2022 — see current edition" with a flag for the specific number.

3. **⚠️ RISKY — T11.L03 / T12.L13 — Reconcile RUS 1753F-401 section cite.** T11.L03 cites §4 for the 0.30 dB splice-loss max; T12.L13 cites §5. One is wrong. Pull the bulletin, confirm the section, and standardize to the correct number across all 42 lessons in this cluster.

4. **⚠️ RISKY — T10.L04 (Burial Depth) — Clarify NEC article for fiber conduit.** The NEC floor table cites "NEC Table 300.5 — 18–24 in" which applies to electrical conductors. Fiber optic conduit (communications wiring) falls under NEC Articles 800/840 with a 12-inch direct-burial minimum. Add a parenthetical or table footnote distinguishing the two. Since RUS 1751F-635 at 36 in governs and renders the NEC floor moot on all RUS projects, the practical impact is low, but the citation as written could mislead a learner on non-RUS builds.

5. **⚠️ RISKY — T12.L03 (OTDR Fundamentals) — Adjust the "typical EIOR" flashcard default value.** The flashcard states "~1.4677 at 1550 nm" as the typical G.652.D EIOR. This is within range for some manufacturers but is more typical of 1310 nm and could lead learners to use it as a default at 1550 nm. Change to "typical range 1.4676–1.4689 at 1550 nm depending on manufacturer — always use the cable datasheet value." This reinforces the lesson's own warning against using defaults.

6. **🟡 PEDAGOGY — T10.L09 (Traffic Control) — Convert the four-zone layout to a TimelineSequence or annotated interactive diagram.** The four work-zone components (advance warning, transition taper, activity area, termination taper) are currently listed as prose. A `TimelineSequence` where the learner advances through the zones along a road, seeing each element in spatial context, would shift this from passive reading to active dual-coding. The stop-work order scenario in the advanced section is good; keep it as the stakes motivator.

7. **🟡 PEDAGOGY — T12.L11 (End-Face Inspection) — Sharpen the Grade C description** and add a concrete quiz question that requires the learner to classify a described defect (e.g., "0.9 µm scratch in Zone A — Grade A or not?") rather than just reciting the grade definitions. Grade A criteria for Zone A (no defects, no contamination) and the Zero-scratch-in-Zone A rule are the most testable facts and should drive at least two quiz items.

8. **🥱 BORING — T10 cluster-wide — Insert a BranchingScenario into at least one of T10.L05–L08.** These construction-execution lessons (conduit pull tension, slack loops, manhole install, pavement restoration) are not deep-read but are likely the most procedurally dense lessons in T10. Adding a scenario where the learner plays an on-site inspector deciding whether to accept or reject an observation — e.g., "the crew's conduit pull gauge shows 800 lbs on a conduit rated for 600 lbs — what do you do?" — would convert passive reading to active decision-making for the content most likely to appear in the field.

9. **🟡 PEDAGOGY — T11.L02 (TIA-598 arithmetic) — Consider moving APC/UPC connector color content to T11.L12 (connector loss).** APC vs. UPC connector physics belongs conceptually with connector-loss measurement, not with fiber-within-tube arithmetic. The current combination creates two distinct skills in one lesson with no dependency between them. If lesson length is manageable (< 25 min) this is low priority; if it's running long, the split improves coherence.

10. **🟡 PEDAGOGY — T12.L15 / T11.L15 / T10.L12 capstone quizzes — Verify interleaved scenario structure.** Capstone quizzes should force retrieval across topics (e.g., a question combining OTDR dead-zone understanding with IOR distance error, or a question requiring the learner to select the right splice-loss threshold for a RUS vs. commercial build). If the capstones merely re-ask individual lesson questions, revise at least 3 items per capstone to require synthesis across ≥2 lessons.
