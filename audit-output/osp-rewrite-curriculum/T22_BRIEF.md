## Executive Summary

T22 covers certification practice exam preparation for two distinct outside-plant specialist certifications:

2. **CFOT (Certified Fiber Optic Technician)** — field technician focus: splicing, testing, safety, documentation (T01–T02, T07–T13, T18).
