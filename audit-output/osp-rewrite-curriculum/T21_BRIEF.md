## 1. FOA CFOS-O Exam Blueprint

**Certification:** Certified Fiber Optic Specialist, Outside Plant (CFOS/O)
- **Sponsoring org:** Fiber Optic Association (FOA)
- **Audience:** Technicians installing outside plant fiber optic cable plants + communications systems
- **Exam format:** Written (70% pass threshold) + hands-on practical (70% pass threshold)
- **Study materials:** FOA Reference Guide to Outside Plant Fiber Optics, Fiber U OSP self-study course

**Exam domains (per FOA KSAs):**
1. Fiber optics fundamentals (theory, light propagation, fiber types)
2. OSP cable types + installation techniques (aerial, underground, conduit, splice cases)
3. Cable preparation + termination (stripping, cleaving, connector attachment)
4. Fusion splicing for concatenation + field repairs
5. OTDR testing + troubleshooting (link characterization, fault location, acceptance)
6. Safety + workmanship standards (OSHA, NESC, cable handling, PPE)
7. Make-ready + design review (existing plant survey, route planning, load calcs)

**Question count estimate:** 100–120 written questions (no public breakdown; typical FOA cert = 100-question pool)
**Time estimate:** 90–120 minutes written exam
**Prerequisites:** CFOT (Certified Fiber Optic Technician) or equivalent field experience strongly recommended
