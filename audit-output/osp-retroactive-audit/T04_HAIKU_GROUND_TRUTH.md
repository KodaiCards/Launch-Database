## RUS Form 307 Verdict

**RESULT: R-2's claim CORRECT. T04.L09 claim INCORRECT.**

**RUS Form 307 is a BID BOND — not a "specifications and drawings checklist for construction package" as claimed in T04.L09.**

Primary sources confirm:
- **Official title:** Form 307 – Bid Bond
- **OMB number:** OMB 0572-0107
- **Purpose:** A penal sum instrument (typically 10% of bid amount) where the Principal and Surety guarantee either contract execution or compensate the Owner for cost differential on re-bidding
- **Primary source:** [Form 307 - Bid Bond (USDA RD)](https://www.rd.usda.gov/files/UP_ET_form_307.pdf)
- **Secondary confirmation:** [Form 307 - Bid Bond - OMB 0572-0107](https://omb.report/icr/200804-0572-002/doc/8582501)
- **Third confirmation:** [RUS Form 307 Bid Bond - OMB 0572-0059](https://omb.report/icr/200710-0572-001/doc/5102901)

**T04.L09 impact:** L09 teaches "RUS Form 307 = specifications and drawings checklist" — this is factually incorrect per USDA RUS. L09 requires correction to teach Form 307 as a bid bond surety instrument, not a construction document checklist.
