### 1f. FCC 47 CFR §1.1306

- **Sources:** Cornell LII at law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/1.1306; eCFR at ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subpart-I/section-1.1306.
- **Confirmed:** 47 CFR §1.1306 is the FCC CE rule. The provision specifically states that "the installation of aerial wire or cable over existing aerial corridors of prior or permitted use" is covered by the CE. The regulation also explicitly covers antenna mounting on existing buildings/structures. It is NOT limited to antenna facilities — it also covers aerial wire and cable in existing corridors. The L02 callout correctly describes this as a CE applicable to "aerial fiber installations on existing pole infrastructure in existing corridors."
- **Technical precision check (RT-α hint §3):** The L02 callout says "Under 47 CFR §1.1306, a categorical exclusion applies to 'construction of new or modified [antenna] facilities' that fall below specified thresholds — and by extension, aerial fiber installations on existing pole infrastructure." This framing is IMPRECISE. The §1.1306 CE for aerial wire/cable in existing corridors is a DIRECT CE for aerial wire, not a CE for antenna facilities "extended" to fiber. The actual §1.1306 text explicitly lists aerial wire/cable in existing corridors as a direct exclusion alongside (not derived from) the antenna CE. The "[antenna] facilities" framing in L02 slightly mischaracterizes the CE's mechanism.
- **Severity:** LOW — the conclusion (fiber qualifies for CE) is correct; the framing of how the CE applies (direct vs. "by extension" from antenna CE) is imprecise but not factually wrong for practical purposes.
