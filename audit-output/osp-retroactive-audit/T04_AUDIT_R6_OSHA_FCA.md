## Stack Snapshot (≤80 words)

T04 teaches field-operations mechanics well. The R-6 OSHA multi-employer + False Claims Act lens finds that T04 systematically omits the federal regulatory compliance framework that surrounds those field operations — specifically: (1) T04 never names the multi-employer worksite doctrine so borrowers know they can be cited as the "controlling employer" even when their own crew doesn't create the hazard; (2) T04's RUS certification content has no False Claims Act context; (3) construction-phase OSHA pre-planning duties (JHA, PRCS inventory, excavation soil classification) are absent as pre-engineering deliverables.
