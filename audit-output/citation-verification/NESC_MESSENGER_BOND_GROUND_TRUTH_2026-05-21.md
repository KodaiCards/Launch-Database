## Primary-Source Findings

### NESC 2023 Edition (C2-2023) Structure

The 2023 NESC organizes rules as follows:

- **Section 9** — Grounding methods for electric supply and communications facilities
  - **Rule 090–097:** Grounding methods (electrode selection, connection methods, supply station grounding, etc.)
  - Rule 96A, 96B, 96C — station grounding, multi-grounded neutral systems
  - **NO "Rule 96F" exists** in Section 9 or any other section of NESC 2023

- **Part 2** — Safety Rules for the Installation and Maintenance of Overhead Electric Supply and Communication Lines
  - **Section 21** — General Requirements (clearances, strength, loading)
  - **Rule 215** — Grounding and bonding
  - **Rule 215D** — **Bonding of messengers, supply neutrals, and metallic poles**

### Messenger Bonding Rule: RULE 215D (2023 Edition)

**VERDICT: Rule 215D is CORRECT for NESC 2023.**

Per authoritative sources (IEEE NESC updates, cooperative/utility guides):
- The previous rule for bonding of communications messengers has been **extended** in the 2023 edition to cover **all messengers, supply neutrals, and metallic poles**
- This extended rule is **Rule 215D** in Part 2, Section 21 (Grounding and Bonding)
- Rule 215D applies to overhead electric supply and communication lines where joint-use structures exist

### Rule 96F Status

**"Rule 96F does NOT exist** in the NESC — not in 2023, not in prior editions (2017, 2012).

Rule 96 designations in Section 9 (Grounding Methods) are:
- Rule 096A — Supply station grounding — general
- Rule 096B — Supply station grounding — specific requirements
- Rule 096C — Multigrounded distribution neutral grounding (four grounds per mile)

No Rule 96D, 96E, or 96F appears in any edition. Section 9 rules cap at 096 + subsections; they do not extend to lettered variants like "96F."
