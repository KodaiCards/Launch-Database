## ⚠ MAJOR FINDING #1 (flag, not fix, per standing instructions) — NEC §770.26 misapplied

The T19 lesson JSX (L01, L06, L08 — heavily in L08's key_terms, Book-vs-Field box, and Q5) cites
**"NEC §770.26"** as the section requiring OSP (unlisted/outside-plant) optical fiber cable to
transition to listed inside-plant cable within **50 feet** of building entry. **This is the wrong
section.** Two independent WebSearch confirmations:

- **NEC Art. 770.26** (NFPA 70) governs **fire-spread prevention / firestopping** at fire-rated
  wall, floor, and ceiling penetrations for optical fiber cable installations — not the 50-foot
  unlisted-cable transition rule.
- **NEC Art. 770.113** is the section that actually contains the 50-foot rule: unlisted (OSP-grade)
  optical fiber cable is permitted inside a building, without being listed, only if the length
  measured from the point of building entrance does not exceed 50 feet (15 m) and the cable
  terminates in an enclosure. (Confirmed via EC&M "The NEC and Optical Fiber Cable and Raceway
  Rules", Mike Holt forums discussion of "Article 770 fiber extending beyond 50'", and up.codes
  mirror of Article 770.)

**My pool questions cite the corrected section (770.113) for the 50-foot rule**, consistent with
the T01 Form-219 / T06 RUS-1751F-635 pattern: pools carry the corrected fact; the lesson JSX
prose is left for a future flip-blocker prose-fix pass (same shape as those two prior fixes).
Recommend routing to Planning/CEO for a T19 prose class-fix dispatch once T19 authoring is
integrated.

## ⚠ MAJOR FINDING #2 (flag, not fix) — RUS Bulletin 1751F-810 misapplied

T19.L01 (Q2 citation) and T19.L06 (key_terms "primary protector" definition, "Book" box, Q1 and
Q4 citations) cite **"RUS Bulletin 1751F-810"** as the source for (a) rural FTTH hut/headend
building-spec practice and (b) the primary-protector/building-entry grounding requirement.
**This is the wrong bulletin.** WebSearch of the RUS Rural Development bulletin index
(rd.usda.gov/resources/regulations/bulletins) confirms:

- **RUS Bulletin 1751F-810** = *"Acceptance Tests for Digital, Stored Program Controlled Central
  Office Equipment"* — a CO switching-equipment acceptance-test bulletin, unrelated to building
  grounding or hut construction.
- **RUS Bulletin 1751F-815** = *"Electrical Protection of Outside Plant"* — this is the bulletin
  that actually covers grounding/protection topics (messenger grounding intervals, lightning/power
  contact protection) referenced by L06's own forward-reference paragraph to T14 ("...and RUS
  1751F-815 requirements" — L06 cites 815 correctly in that one spot, then 810 incorrectly
  everywhere else in the same lesson — an internal inconsistency).
- **RUS Bulletin 1751F-805** = *"Electrical Protection At Customer Locations"* — a related but
  distinct bulletin (customer-premises installer scope, not CO/hut building-entry scope).

**My pool questions cite RUS 1751F-815** for building-entry grounding/primary-protector content,
matching L06's own (correct, buried) forward-reference. Same "flag, don't fix" treatment as
Finding #1 — recommend a combined T19 prose class-fix dispatch (770.26→770.113 AND 1751F-810→
1751F-815) once authoring is integrated, same shape as the T01/T06 precedent.
