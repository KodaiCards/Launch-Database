## Content-accuracy finding (flagged, not fixed, per dispatch instructions)

`T04-L07`'s lesson JSX cites **47 CFR 32.2230** as "Telecommunications Plant Under Construction."
Independent verification found 32.2230 is actually titled "transmission" (radio systems/circuit
equipment). The correct account for Plant Under Construction is **47 CFR 32.2003**. This is a real
citation error in the pre-existing lesson prose, corrected in the pool questions and flagged here —
consistent with the pattern of catches across T01 (5 errors), T02 (1), and now T04 (1).
