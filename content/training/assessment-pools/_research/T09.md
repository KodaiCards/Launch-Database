## Live regulatory-change finding (verified independently, not just carried from the lesson JSX)

The T09-L01 lesson JSX itself flags a critical, current regulatory change: **CEQ's NEPA implementing
regulations at 40 CFR Parts 1500-1508 were removed from the CFR.** I independently verified this via
WebSearch rather than trusting the lesson's self-flag:
- Interim final rule published Feb 25, 2025, removing 40 CFR 1500-1508, **effective April 11, 2025**
  (following Executive Order 14154, "Unleashing American Energy," Jan 20, 2025, which revoked E.O.
  11991 — the order that had originally directed CEQ to issue binding NEPA regulations).
- That removal was **finalized effective January 8, 2026** (Federal Register final rule).
- Practical effect: federal agencies now implement NEPA through their own agency-specific procedures
  rather than a single CEQ-wide regulatory scheme. **Pool questions cite NEPA to its statutory
  authority (42 USC 4321 et seq. / 42 USC 4332) rather than to the now-rescinded 40 CFR 1500-1508**,
  even though the CEO's inc10 dispatch listed "NEPA 40 CFR 1500-1508" as a suggested citation target —
  flagging this discrepancy for the CEO since citing rescinded regulations as current authority would
  itself be a citation-gate violation.
- **PASS (independently verified, corrects the dispatch's suggested citation).**

Similarly verified: **7 CFR Part 1970** (the former RUS/Rural Development NEPA procedures, including
the old §1970.54 categorical exclusion) **is now "[Reserved]"** — consolidated into **7 CFR Part 1b**,
per a USDA final rule **effective April 3, 2026**. This matches the T09-L02/L11 lesson JSX's own
citation to "7 CFR Part 1b (eff. April 3, 2026; replaced 7 CFR §1970.54)." **PASS.**
