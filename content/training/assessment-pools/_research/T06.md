## MAJOR FINDING — systemic RUS bulletin misapplication across T06 (flagged, not fixed in prose)

The pre-existing T06 lesson JSX cites **RUS Bulletin 1751F-635** pervasively (in nearly every one of
the 11 lessons) as the primary bulletin governing UNDERGROUND plant design — burial depth (L02),
pedestal/slack-storage spacing (L08), innerduct traceability (L10), design QA (L11), etc.

**Independent WebSearch verification (multiple corroborating searches) found this is WRONG:**
- **RUS Bulletin 1751F-635 is titled "Aerial Plant Construction"** — a bulletin scoped to overhead
  pole-attachment/lashed-cable construction, confirmed via two independent searches (rd.usda.gov
  bulletin PDF listing; a third corroborating source describes it as covering "aerial plant for
  both filled copper cables and filled fiber optic cables... attaching cables to utility poles").
- **The bulletin actually scoped to underground plant is RUS Bulletin 1751F-643**, titled
  "Underground Plant Design" (effective 2002-08-28) — confirmed via WebSearch (rd.usda.gov bulletin
  PDF listing, describing coverage of "design and construction of underground plant facilities...
  poured-in-place underground cable vaults... conduit, duct, and innerduct arrangements").
- A related bulletin, **RUS 1751F-640 "Design of Buried Plant — Physical Considerations,"** also
  surfaced as a candidate underground-scoped bulletin in the search results but was not the one the
  T06 lesson JSX's L10 title itself points to (L10 is titled "RUS 1751F-643 Innerduct Standard,"
  which IS the correctly-scoped bulletin per the finding above).

**Practical effect:** every pool question in this topic that needs a "which RUS bulletin governs
underground plant design" citation uses the CORRECTED bulletin (1751F-643), not the erroneous
1751F-635 the lesson prose repeatedly cites. This is flagged for the CEO as a systemic,
topic-wide citation error in the pre-existing lesson JSX — not something to fix in the pool JSON
alone, since the JSX prose itself (across most of T06's 11 lessons) needs the same correction.
This is analogous in scope to the RUS Form 219 pattern found across T01, but spans MORE lessons
within a single topic (T06) than any prior single-topic finding this session.
