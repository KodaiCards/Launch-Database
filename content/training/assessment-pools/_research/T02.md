## Verification method (same as T01/T18, D024)

`WebFetch` to primary-source hosts (itu.int, iec.ch, ecfr.gov) is blocked by the org egress proxy
policy (established in the T01 pass). `WebSearch` was used for every citation below, checked against
multiple independent snippets (ITU/IEC standard summary pages, vendor datasheets, and independent
technical references) for numeric accuracy and standard/section identity. T02 is physics-heavy;
pure-arithmetic questions (dB math, link-budget subtraction, DGD = D x sqrt(L) calculations) are
independently re-derived by hand in this log rather than "cited," since they are self-verifying
logarithm/algebra, not claims resting on an external authority.
