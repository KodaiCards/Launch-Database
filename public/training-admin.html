Content Access — Presets

Presets are reusable, named content sets you can keep handy (e.g. "OSP crew", "ISP"). Day-to-day visibility is driven by Publish (what's live), the New-user default (what new signups see), and per-person grant / revoke in each person's row.

Check what this preset includes. Checking a track or subject includes everything under it; expand to pick individual lessons.

Publish — what's live to trainees

Check a lesson to publish it (make it available at all); uncheck to take it down. Use a subject/track Show/Hide all to publish in bulk. Nothing un-published is ever visible to a trainee. (Admins always see everything.)
